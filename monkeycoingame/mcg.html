mcg

Move the bloon with WASD or Arrow Keys, tap on the screen to interact with upgrades/health-packs. "Space" to Dash. This is 1.0 of Monkey Coin Game.


I made this game during 8th grade in Scratch for some pals of mine-- I recently rebuilt the whole thing for this website (and also to fix the bugs).


This IS compatible with IPads! Although I encourage not to use fullscreen.


The goal of the game is to survive the monkey onslaught as long as you can-- you'll accrue coins over time. Use these coins to buy upgrades and survive even longer!


I will probably add more content to this game as updates-- it's very easy to do so. Also, cool seaver made the cool thumbnail.


There is currently: 1 known bug. (If you are popped near the bottom of the screen, you could clip out of bounds.)
